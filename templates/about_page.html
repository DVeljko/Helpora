About Helpora

Helping people connect through generosity.

Helpora is a platform created to connect people who need support with people who are willing to help.

Our Mission

Make helping easier and more transparent.

Helpora gives people a place where they can create campaigns for medical treatment, financial difficulties, housing problems and other situations where support is needed.

Our goal is to make every campaign clear, organized and easy to understand, so donors can see who they are helping and why their support matters.

Why Helpora?

✓
Transparent Donations

Donations can be recorded and connected to individual campaigns.

✓
Different Types of Help

Campaigns can support medical treatment, housing needs and financial difficulties.

✓
Simple and Accessible

People can quickly browse campaigns and choose who they want to support.
